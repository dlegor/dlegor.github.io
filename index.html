Hello, I'm Daniel Legorreta. I have spent over a decade working on data projects, taking on various roles such as data scientist, data engineer, ML engineer, MLOps engineer, and data analyst. Consequently, I do not limit myself to a single label, given the diversity of positions and projects I have handled. My experience ranges from building real-time forecasting systems to developing document data extraction solutions (including RAG systems) and AI agents. While I have a preference for certain technologies, I am passionate about solving complex problems, especially those that can have a positive impact on people's lives.

My core expertise includes:

  • Advanced NLP and LLMs for large-scale information retrieval and text comparison.
  • Scalable forecasting models in distributed environments.
  • Anomaly detection systems.
  • Computer vision and deep learning systems.

I constantly explore new techniques, algorithms, and technologies related to both model development and their deployment into production. I also enjoy sharing my knowledge through teaching and mentoring sessions whenever time permits.
